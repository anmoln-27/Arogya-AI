Example: CAD Design & Mechanical Specs
Design Iteration: Tiller mount plate thickness increased from 3.0mm to 4.5mm based on FEA fatigue analysis, eliminating stress hot spots under cyclic torque.
Figure: SolidWorks exploded assembly of active rotary weeding module & 4-bar linkage
Mechanical Validation & FEA Stress Report
Chassis Members: 30x30x2mm Aluminum 6061-T6 box extrusion; total frame mass 6.2 kg; yield strength 276 MPa.
Rotary Tiller Blades: Hardened EN45 Spring Steel with oil-quenched temper (48 HRC) to withstand quartz abrasion and stone impacts.
FEA Loading Simulation: 350 N dynamic shock load applied vertically to suspension pivot bracket (simulating a 50mm ditch drop at 1.0 m/s).
Stress & Safety Factor: Peak Von Mises stress of 114.2 MPa concentrated at pivot fillet; minimum Factor of Safety (FoS) = 2.41.
Max Deflection: 0.42 mm along cantilevered drive axis, well within deep-groove ball bearing angular misalignment tolerances.

==================== SLIDE 18 (ppt/slides/slide18.xml) ====================
Example: Technical Challenges & Risks
Empirical Verification: Benchtop clay test rig showed a 94% reduction in mechanical rotational drag after installing the PTFE scraper combs.
Observed Risk: High-moisture (>25%) black cotton soil adheres to rotary cultivator teeth, causing rotational binding within 12 minutes of continuous furrow tilling.
Mitigation: Installed stationary low-friction PTFE scraper blades offset 1.5mm from the tine arc to dynamically peel away clay buildup on every rotation.
Observed Risk: Hub motor temperatures reached 68°C in simulation during sustained noon operations at 38°C ambient Maharashtra field heat.
Mitigation: Retrofitted extruded radial aluminum heatsinks directly coupled via 3.0 W/mK silicone thermal pads, stabilizing motor casing temps below 47°C.
ISO 4254-1 Compliance: 2mm sheet steel blade shrouds eliminate projectile pebble hazards.
IP65 Ingress Sealing: Gasket-sealed ASA electronics bay with IP68 PG7 cable glands tested against direct pressurized spray.
IEC 62133: BMS equipped with cell-level thermal sensors and overcharge isolation.
Bottleneck: Wet Soil Clogging
Bottleneck: Thermal Throttling
