📶 The Wireless Disconnect (0% RF on Nucleo)
Smart-ASHA's primary clinical interface is a 100% offline Flutter app in Airplane Mode streaming live 60 FPS pulse waves and thermal heatmaps over BLE 5.0. All 7 Nucleo boards lack on-chip wireless, forcing the addition of an external radio module (ironically an ESP32), increasing board failure points.
⚙ Asymmetric Dual-Core vs Single-Core Jitter
The ESP32-S3 dedicates Core 0 strictly to 2D-CNN inference while Core 1 continuously polls the MAX30105 at 400 kHz. On single-core STM32 boards, running neural inference locks the CPU for 90–280 ms, dropping photoplethysmogram peak samples without complex DMA circular buffers.
🏆 Economic & Ergonomic Summary
The 7Semi ESP32-S3 integrates MCU, 4G LTE Cat-1, GNSS, and BLE into a compact 48 × 38 mm footprint that fits inside our 210 × 125 × 45 mm IP54 handheld sleeve. Replacing it with any Nucleo board inflates the BOM by ₹2,500–₹3,700 and exceeds the internal CAD enclosure volume.
